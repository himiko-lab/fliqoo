# Privacy Policy for Fliqoo

**Effective Date:** June 2, 2026

Himiko Lab built the Fliqoo app as a Free app. This service is provided by Himiko Lab at no cost and is intended for use as is. This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our Service.

If you choose to use our Service, then you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy. We prioritize your privacy and strictly limit the data we process.

## 1. Local Data Storage & Account Policy
Fliqoo is designed as an offline-first, privacy-centric application. **We do not require you to create a user account on our servers.** All of your core financial data—including balances, transactions, budgets, savings, and bills—are stored entirely locally on your device's internal storage (SQLite/Room Database). We (Himiko Lab) do not collect, transmit, or have any access to your personal financial records.

## 2. Google Drive Backup Integration
Fliqoo provides an optional feature to back up and restore your local database using your personal Google Drive account.
* If you choose to enable this, the app will request access to your Google Drive strictly to upload (backup) and download (restore) the specific Fliqoo database files (`.db` and `-wal`).
* We do not have access to your Google credentials, nor do we access any other personal files stored in your Google Drive. 
* This backup process occurs directly between your device and Google's servers.

## 3. Fliqoo AI (Generative AI Features)
Fliqoo utilizes Generative AI to provide personalized financial insights. 
* When you interact with Fliqoo AI, a temporary summary of your local financial data (such as total balance, upcoming bills, or active budgets) may be securely transmitted to the AI provider (Google Gemini API) to generate contextual advice.
* This data is used transiently solely for the purpose of generating the response. It is **not** stored on our servers, **not** used to train public AI models, and **not** shared with unauthorized third parties.

## 4. Location Data
Fliqoo may request access to your device's location (foreground). 
* This permission is used exclusively for a quality-of-life feature: automatically filling in the location name when you record a transaction.
* Your location data is processed locally or transiently to fetch the location name. **We never track, store, or transmit your location history to our servers.**

## 5. Log Data and Analytics
We want to inform you that whenever you use our Service, in a case of an error or app lag, we collect data and information (through third-party products) on your phone called Log Data. This Log Data may include information such as your device Internet Protocol ("IP") address, device name, operating system version, the configuration of the app when utilizing our Service, the time and date of your use of the Service, and other statistics. This is used strictly to improve app performance and fix bugs.

## 6. Advertising
Currently, the Fliqoo application does not display any third-party advertisements. Your data is not sold or shared with advertising networks.

## 7. Device Permissions
For the app to function optimally, we may request the following permissions:
* **Notifications:** To send you reminders for upcoming bills and financial goals.
* **Location:** To assist with auto-filling transaction locations.
* **Internet:** Required for syncing backups with Google Drive and communicating with the Fliqoo AI service.

## 8. Security
We value your trust in providing us your information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. But remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
